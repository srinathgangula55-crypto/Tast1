Silkworm Growth Stages

Complete silkworm lifecycle from young worms to cocoon formation.

1st Stage

Young worms begin feeding on tender mulberry leaves under controlled conditions.

2nd & 3rd Stage

Active feeding stage with increased nutrition and continuous growth monitoring.

4th & 5th Stage

Fully grown worms consume large quantities of leaves before cocoon spinning.

Cocoon Formation

Mature silkworms stop feeding and naturally spin premium silk cocoons.

Cocoon Processing

Careful cocoon management ensures superior silk quality and sustainable production.

Cocoon Harvesting

Healthy cocoons are collected carefully after complete spinning process.

Silk Extraction

Premium silk threads are extracted using eco-friendly and hygienic methods.

Temperature Control

Maintaining proper temperature and humidity is essential for healthy silkworm growth and premium cocoon production.

Early Stage Temperature

Young silkworm larvae require warm and stable temperatures between 26°C to 28°C for healthy feeding and growth during initial stages.

Humidity Maintenance

Proper humidity levels between 45% to 80% are carefully maintained to support healthy silkworm growth, smooth molting, and disease-free cocoon development.

Late Age Rearing Care

Late-stage silkworms require cool airflow and proper ventilation systems to support healthy cocoon spinning activities.
