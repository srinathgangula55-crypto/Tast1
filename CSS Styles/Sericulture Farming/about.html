Our Farm Story

Our sericulture farming journey started with a vision to promote organic silk production using sustainable mulberry cultivation and healthy silkworm rearing systems.

We combine traditional farming knowledge with modern agricultural practices to produce premium quality silk naturally.

Daily Farm Activities

Essential activities performed every day to maintain healthy silkworm growth and premium cocoon production.

Leaf Harvesting

Fresh mulberry leaves are harvested carefully for healthy silkworm feeding and balanced nutrition.

Worm Feeding

Silkworms are fed regularly with nutrient-rich mulberry leaves for healthy development and cocoon growth.

Bed Cleaning

Rearing beds are cleaned regularly to maintain hygiene and prevent silkworm diseases naturally.

Healthy Farming Environment

Natural farming methods create a healthy and eco-friendly environment for silk production.

Organic Farming

Chemical-free mulberry cultivation improves leaf quality and soil fertility.

Natural Ventilation

Healthy airflow and proper temperature management support worm development.

Eco-Friendly Methods

Sustainable farming protects the environment and improves silk production quality.

Future Farming Goals

Expanding sustainable silk farming for future generations and eco-friendly agriculture.

Farmer Support
