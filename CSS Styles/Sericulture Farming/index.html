About Sericulture

Organic silk farming combines traditional techniques with modern farming methods.

1st Stage of Worms

First-stage silkworms are newly hatched young worms that begin feeding on tender mulberry leaves.

2nd Stage of Worms

Second-stage silkworms are slightly grown worms that consume more mulberry leaves for faster development and healthy growth.

3rd Stage of Worms

Third-stage silkworms are actively growing worms that require increased feeding and proper care for strong cocoon formation.

4th Stage of Worms

Fourth-stage silkworms are mature growing worms that consume large quantities of mulberry leaves to prepare for cocoon development.

5th Stage of Worms

Fifth-stage silkworms are fully grown worms that eat heavily before spinning high-quality silk cocoons.

Final Rearing Stage

Final rearing stage is the phase where mature silkworms stop feeding and begin spinning silk cocoons for pupation.

Farming Process

Step-by-step process involved in one complete silkworm batch from day 1 to cocoon harvesting.

Day 1-5

1st Stage Worms

New batch silkworms are feed with tender chopped mulberry leaves under controlled temperature and humidity conditions.

Day 6-10

2nd Stage Worms

Young worms begin active feeding and require regular leaf supply with proper bed cleaning for healthy growth.

Day 11-15

3rd Stage Worms

Silkworms consume more mulberry leaves and develop rapidly with increased nutritional requirements.

Day 16-19

4th Stage Worms

Worms grow larger and require sufficient feeding space, ventilation, and fresh mulberry leaf supply.

Day 20-23

5th Stage Worms

Fully grown silkworms consume maximum quantity of leaves before entering cocoon spinning stage.

Day 24-25

Cocoon Formation

Mature worms stop feeding and start spinning silk cocoons naturally in mounting structures.
